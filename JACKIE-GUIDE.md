# Editing your website — a quick guide

You don't need to know any code to update your site. Everything you'd want
to change lives in one page.

## Logging in

1. Go to **yourwebsite.com/admin** (Philip will tell you the exact address).
2. Type in your password and click **Sign In**.

That's it — you're now on the editing page.

## The editing page

Everything is grouped into boxes, top to bottom:

- **Site Title** — the name shown in the top corner of your site.
- **Homepage Introduction** — the big welcome text people see first, plus
  the featured photo next to it. Visitors can click that photo to see it
  bigger.
- **The Collection** — every piece on your site. Each one can have as many
  photos as you want, plus a title, a category (like "Embroidery" or
  "Stop Motion"), and a year. Clicking a piece on your live site now opens
  its own page showing every photo you've added for it.
- **Meet the Artist** — your bio, plus a photo of yourself if you'd like one
  shown alongside it.
- **Field Notes** — your blog-style feed for progress updates, with a title,
  a date, some writing, and as many photos as you want per post. Your
  homepage always shows your most recent post automatically, including its
  first photo, with a link to the full Field Notes page where all your posts
  live.
- **Contact Info** — your email, Instagram handle, and (optional) a link to
  your YouTube channel, which shows as a small icon under "Inquiries" on
  your site.
- **Footer** — the small copyright line at the very bottom.

## To change any text

Just click into the box and type — delete what's there and write your own
words. When you're done with everything you want to change, scroll all the
way to the bottom and click the big **Save Changes** button.

Nothing changes on your live site until you click that button.

## To add a new piece to your collection

1. Scroll to **The Collection**.
2. Click **+ Add a New Piece** at the bottom of that section — a new blank
   row appears.
3. Type in the title, category, and year.
4. Click **Add photos** next to it. You can pick just one photo, or hold
   Ctrl (or Cmd on a Mac) — or on your phone, just tap several in a row — to
   select multiple photos at once. All of them will be added to that piece.
5. Scroll down and click **Save Changes**.

## To add more photos to an existing piece

Find that piece's row, click **Add photos**, and pick one or more new
photos. They'll be added alongside any photos already there — nothing gets
replaced. Click **Save Changes** when you're done.

## To remove one photo from a piece

Find the small thumbnail of that photo and click the little **×** button in
its corner. The thumbnail will disappear from the row. Click **Save
Changes** at the bottom to make it official.

## To remove a piece from your site

Find its row and click the red **Remove This Piece** button. It'll
disappear from the list. Click **Save Changes** at the bottom to make it
official. (If you remove one by accident, just don't save — closing the
page without saving undoes nothing.)

## To choose which photo shows first (the "cover" photo)

Whichever photo is first in a piece's row is the one shown on the gallery
card on your homepage — the other photos are still there, just visible once
someone clicks into that piece. To change which one is first, use the small
**‹** and **›** arrows on each photo's thumbnail to move it earlier or
later, until the one you want is in the front. Click **Save Changes** when
you're happy with the order. Field Notes posts work the same way — the
first photo in a post is the one shown in the "Latest Field Note" preview
on your homepage.

## To write a new Field Notes post

1. Scroll to **Field Notes**.
2. Click **+ Add a New Field Note** at the bottom of that section — a new
   blank post appears, with today's date already filled in.
3. Give it a title and write about what you're working on in the big text
   box.
4. Click **Add photos** to attach progress pictures — pick one or several at
   once, just like adding photos to a piece.
5. Scroll down and click **Save Changes**.

Your newest post (by date) automatically becomes the one shown on your
homepage — you don't need to do anything special to "feature" it. All of
your posts, newest first, live on the full Field Notes page, which people
reach by clicking **Field Notes** in the menu at the top of your site.

## To edit or add photos to an existing Field Notes post

Find that post and change its title, date, or writing directly — or click
**Add photos** to add more pictures to it. To remove a single photo, click
the little **×** on its thumbnail. Click **Save Changes** when you're done.

## To remove a Field Notes post

Find it and click the red **Remove This Post** button, then **Save
Changes**.

## To add or change your photo in "Meet the Artist"

Scroll to **Meet the Artist**, click **Choose File** next to "Your photo,"
and pick a picture. It'll show up beside your bio on the live site. Upload
a new one anytime to replace it. Click **Save Changes** when you're done.

## To add your YouTube link

Scroll to **Contact Info** and paste your channel's link (something like
`https://youtube.com/@yourhandle`) into the **YouTube channel link** box.
A small YouTube icon will appear under "Inquiries" on your site that links
straight to your channel. Leave that box empty and the icon just won't show
up.

## A couple of notes

- Photos can be pretty much any size — the site will resize them to fit
  automatically. If a photo looks huge on your phone or computer before
  uploading, that's fine.
- If you make a mistake, you can always come back and fix it — nothing is
  permanent until you click **Save Changes**, and even after saving you can
  just edit it again.
- If you ever forget your password, ask Philip — he can reset it for you.
